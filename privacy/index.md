<section class="page-hero"><div class="wrap narrow"><span class="eyebrow">Privacy</span><h1>Information should stay in the context where it was shared.</h1><p class="lede">This page describes the intentionally limited public-site handling for the current GitHub Pages build.</p></div></section>
<section class="section"><div class="wrap prose"><h2>Contact information</h2><p>If you call or email SteadyMark, the information you provide is handled through those communication channels for the purpose of responding to your property need.</p><h2>Request Work</h2><p>The current static form prepares an email in your own email client rather than storing a request in a site database. Attachments remain under the control of the sender and email provider. Do not include passwords, access codes, resident information, or other sensitive material.</p><h2>Analytics and third parties</h2><p>This launch build does not transmit form fields or request payloads to analytics services. No chat, scheduling widget, review feed, or advertising tracker is included.</p><h2>Configuration note</h2><p>Storage, notification, retention, and upload-provider decisions remain unresolved operational configuration. If a server-side Request Work endpoint is added before launch, this page must be updated to reflect its actual handling.</p></div></section>
