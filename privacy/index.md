<section class="page-hero"><div class="wrap narrow"><span class="eyebrow">Privacy</span><h1>Information should stay in the context where it was shared.</h1><p class="lede">This page describes the intentionally limited public-site handling for the current GitHub Pages build.</p></div></section>
<section class="section"><div class="wrap prose"><h2>Contact information</h2><p>If you call or email SteadyMark, the information you provide is handled through those communication channels for the purpose of responding to your property need.</p><h2>Request Work</h2><p>The Request Work page is an orientation and intake surface. Calling SteadyMark is the clearest next step; the email option prepares information in your own email client. Attachments remain under the control of the sender and email provider. Do not include passwords, access codes, resident information, or other sensitive material.</p><h2>Analytics and third parties</h2><p>This launch build does not transmit form fields or request payloads to analytics services. No chat, scheduling widget, review feed, or advertising tracker is included.</p></div></section>
