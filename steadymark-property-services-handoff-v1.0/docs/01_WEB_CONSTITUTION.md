# SteadyMark Web Constitution v1.0

## Purpose

`steadymarkservices.com` exists to establish SteadyMark Property Services as a credible, accountable property-services operation and make it easy for a person with a property need to understand fit and request work.

The fundamental visitor sequence is:

**Understand → Verify fit → Establish trust → Describe the property need → Request work**

The site is not primarily an advertising funnel, portfolio gallery, contractor résumé, or generic handyman brochure.

## Business model

SteadyMark is organized around four operating territories:

- Make-Readies & Turns
- Maintenance & Property Support
- Repairs & Punch Work
- Recurring & Contract Support

Smaller residential repair work may fit naturally within these territories, but the company is not framed as “we do anything” handyman labor.

## Accountability

Responsibility must remain visible. Robert is the founder and accountable human behind SteadyMark. The website may communicate sufficient field capacity for accepted scope, including trusted scope-matched support, but must not manufacture the appearance of a large anonymous company.

## Audience

The strongest strategic audience includes property managers, landlords, investors, multifamily operators, and owners with recurring needs. Homeowners with legitimate individual property needs remain welcome.

## Truth before persuasion

Prefer specificity over adjectives. Show the condition, describe the scope, explain responsibility, and show the resulting state where documentary evidence exists. Generic words such as “quality,” “best,” “trusted,” and “professional” may not substitute for evidence.

## Evidence architecture

Three visually similar but semantically distinct things must remain separate:

1. **Representative capability imagery** — illustrates the kind of property, condition, or task SteadyMark may help with.
2. **Robert working imagery** — establishes human and operational context.
3. **Project Records** — future documentary evidence of real completed work.

Representative imagery may never silently become a named case study, testimonial, job history, or proof of a specific completed project.

## Synthetic imagery boundary

Synthetic and reference-derived imagery may establish service recognition while the real photography library grows. Its role is to illustrate **what SteadyMark helps with**, not falsely document **what SteadyMark did at a specific property**.

The architecture must make later replacement by real photography easy without redesigning page structure.

## Public surface architecture

The four primary public surfaces are:

- Home
- Services / What SteadyMark Helps With
- About
- Request Work

No root page should be created merely for symmetry or apparent scale.

## Home

Home owns orientation and confidence. It must quickly answer: Who is SteadyMark? What kinds of property needs fit? Who is it for? How does SteadyMark operate? Who is accountable? What should the visitor do next?

Primary action: **Request Work**.

## Services

Services should describe property situations, not merely list trades. The four Service Lines are primary. Representative Scopes provide concrete examples beneath them.

## About

About establishes accountability, operating character, and fit. Robert should appear primarily in working context. The page must not imply either that Robert personally performs every task or that SteadyMark is a large staffed corporation.

## Request Work

Request Work is an intake instrument. The customer should be able to describe a property-level problem in ordinary language. The site should gather only what is useful for understanding and responding to scope.

Canonical contact information:

- service@steadymarkservices.com
- (210) 710-4309

## Service boundaries

The website must not create capability claims merely because a generated image depicts a trade. Copy and imagery must distinguish ordinary property support from work that requires licenses, permits, specialty contractors, or regulated authority.

## Voice

The voice is **steady · capable · organized · practical · human · direct**. Prefer ordinary language such as “Tell us what the property needs” over generic marketing language.

## Visual identity

Canonical palette:

- Working Surface `#F4EFEA`
- Graphite `#2E2E2E`
- Clay / Oxide `#B2553C`
- Stone `#C9C2B8`
- Steel `#8A9399`

Manrope is the production communication family. The custom STEADYMARK wordmark is separate canonical artwork.

## Registration-mark grammar

The SteadyMark registration gesture is a structural reference mark. It may indicate Reference, Connection, or Completion / resulting condition. It is not a rating, quality badge, approval seal, generic checkmark, or decorative pattern.

## Photography

Photography is property-first and documentary in character. Favor believable residential and multifamily environments, ordinary materials, working conditions, practical corrections, natural light, and restrained tools. Avoid luxury-remodel fantasy, exaggerated damage, and stock-photo contractor posing.

## Responsive behavior

Mobile is a deliberate composition of the same architecture, not a cropped desktop page. Content priority remains stable while spatial relationships adapt.

## Accessibility

Meaning may not depend solely on color, imagery, hover state, animation, or visual position. Semantic HTML, labels, keyboard operation, focus visibility, error messaging, reduced-motion behavior, and meaningful alt text are production requirements.

## Performance

Prefer static/server-rendered content, efficient CSS, lightweight SVG, responsive images, lazy loading below the fold, restrained JavaScript, and minimal third-party dependencies.

## Motion

Motion may support orientation, state change, reveal, and transition. It should never exist simply to make the site feel expensive.

## Local credibility

SteadyMark should establish the geography it actually serves without keyword stuffing or fake offices. Search should discover the real business rather than reshape it.

## Analytics

Measurement should answer practical questions about discovery, service interest, Request Work progress, and qualified inquiries. Analytics should never justify manipulative funnel behavior.

## Deterministic implementation

> **Deterministic by default, probabilistic by exception.**

There is no reason to embed a customer-facing AI chatbot merely because AI exists. Forms, validation, navigation, routing, and service matching should use explicit predictable rules.

## Authority order

1. Web Constitution
2. Site / Information Architecture
3. Page and interaction contracts
4. Canonical content/business data
5. Visual/identity specifications
6. Production assets
7. Approved composition references
8. Generated specimen copy/details

## Governing design fingerprint

> Understand the real system first. Give important things a purpose and a home. Expose enough structure to make truth legible. Use evidence over adjectives. Keep responsibility visible. Allow depth without requiring it. Let visual character emerge from the subject. Keep the shortest path to the visitor’s real objective obvious.
