# SteadyMark Page Contracts v1.0

## Global rule

Every section must either orient, establish fit, establish trust, deepen understanding, or move the visitor toward an appropriate next action.

Every public page inherits the canonical header/footer, Manrope typography, approved palette, registration-mark grammar, accessibility behavior, responsive rules, and asset/provenance boundaries.

## Home `/`

**Job:** establish SteadyMark, establish fit, communicate the operating model, and route the visitor to Services or Request Work.

Sequence:

1. Hero
2. What SteadyMark Helps With
3. Coordination advantage
4. Representative situations
5. Robert/accountability bridge
6. Recurring-support signal
7. How it works
8. Final Request Work CTA

Hero uses `SM-HERO-01`. Primary action is **Request Work**; secondary path is Services.

Home may feature three Representative Scopes:

- Vacant Unit Turn
- Multi-Item Punch Scope
- Recurring Multi-Property Support

The “How it works” progression is:

**Tell us what the property needs → SteadyMark reviews the scope → Fit and next steps are clarified → Accepted work is coordinated**

Do not add fake testimonials, stats, pricing, logo clouds, or portfolio claims.

## Services `/services/`

**Job:** let visitors recognize the kinds of property needs that fit SteadyMark.

Public heading: **What SteadyMark Helps With**.

Sequence:

**Orientation → Four Service Lines → Representative Scopes → Fit / boundaries → Request Work**

Each Service Line receives a substantial editorial section rather than a tiny SaaS card.

## Make-Readies & Turns

**Job:** communicate movement from current condition toward a defined ready condition.

Primary asset: `SM-SVC-01`.

Primary Representative Scope: **Vacant Unit Turn**.

Supporting details may include wall/finish, door/hardware, cabinet, flooring transition, and working-material imagery.

Do not imply leasing, tenant placement, property management, inspection authority, or a guaranteed independent definition of “rent-ready.”

## Maintenance & Property Support

**Job:** show how SteadyMark helps keep properties functional, presentable, and attended to over time.

Primary asset: `SM-SVC-02`.

Primary Representative Scope: **Rental Maintenance Visit**.

Secondary relationship: **Single Residential Repair**.

This page should feel routine rather than dramatic.

## Repairs & Punch Work

**Job:** show that a defined repair or a collection of smaller corrective items can be organized into one understandable scope.

Primary asset: `SM-SVC-03`.

Primary Representative Scope: **Multi-Item Punch Scope**.

Secondary: **Single Residential Repair**.

Core idea: several unrelated property issues can still form one coordinated request.

## Recurring & Contract Support

**Job:** establish the possibility of an ongoing SteadyMark relationship for repeated property needs.

Use `SM-SVC-04` and `SM-PROP-01`.

Primary Representative Scope: **Recurring Multi-Property Support**.

Do not invent contract tiers, subscription packages, guaranteed turnaround, or dedicated account-manager structures.

## About `/about/`

**Job:** establish human accountability and explain how SteadyMark operates.

Sequence:

**Why SteadyMark exists → Robert → How capacity works → Accountability → Who SteadyMark supports → Trust facts → Request Work**

Robert is presented as Founder. Prefer working-context imagery. Do not imply Robert personally performs every task or invent a large staff.

## Request Work `/request-work/`

**Job:** convert an uncertain property need into enough structured information for SteadyMark to determine the appropriate next step.

Opening question: **What does the property need?**

Initial choices:

- Repair or smaller job
- Make-ready / unit turn
- Multi-item / punch scope
- Recurring / multi-property support
- Not sure

Common essentials:

- name
- phone
- email
- preferred contact method
- property location/context
- description
- desired timing
- optional photos/files

Submission means **Request received**, not booked, accepted, quoted, or scheduled.

## Request Received `/request-work/received/`

Simple acknowledgement only. Explain what happens next and provide canonical phone/email. No upsell. `noindex`.

## Privacy `/privacy/`

Must reflect actual technical handling of form data, uploads, analytics, contact information, and third-party infrastructure. Do not invent legal language before infrastructure decisions exist.

## 404

Correct HTTP status and simple recovery via Home, Services, and Request Work.

## Asset responsibility

- Home: hero, selected service anchors, selected scope imagery, one Robert image
- Services: four service anchors + relevant details
- Service Line pages: one principal anchor + scope visuals + 1–3 detail images
- About: Robert working-context imagery primarily
- Request Work: minimal or no photography; the form is the protagonist

## Responsive rule

Information order is semantic, not decorative. DOM order must preserve logical reading order even when desktop layout alternates image and text positions.
