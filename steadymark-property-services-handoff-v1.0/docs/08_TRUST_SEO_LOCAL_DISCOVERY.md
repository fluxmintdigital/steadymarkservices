# SteadyMark Trust, SEO & Local Discovery Contract v1.0

## Governing rule

> Search should discover the structure of the real business, not cause us to invent a different business for search.

## Trust architecture

Trust grows through:

**clear identity → visible accountability → understandable services → real contact information → defined process → accurate geography → legitimate credentials → genuine reviews → real Project Records**

Do not substitute fake testimonials, job counts, years in business, star graphics, trust seals, “best” claims, or fabricated badges.

## Address/service-area truth

If SteadyMark is a service-area business without a staffed customer-facing office, do not manufacture a storefront signal. Do not invent satellite offices, virtual offices, mailbox branches, or neighborhood locations.

## Geography

Use a canonical ServiceGeography object. Natural language may state San Antonio-area context once exact coverage is confirmed. Avoid repeated city-name stuffing.

## No thin location pages

Do not generate ZIP/city landing pages by template. Geographic routes should emerge only when unique operational or documentary content justifies them.

## Search intent

The core site naturally serves brand intent, service intent, problem intent, and local intent. Human-useful structure is primary; search engines receive the same structure.

## Titles and descriptions

Unique factual titles for each route, generally `Page Subject | SteadyMark Property Services`. Avoid keyword chains. Meta descriptions summarize actual page value and next step.

## Canonicals/indexing

Use one authoritative HTTPS hostname. Canonical URLs for indexable public pages. `/request-work/received/`, staging, private files, operational endpoints, and internal surfaces should not be indexed.

## Sitemap/robots

Generate XML sitemap from canonical route data. Include only intended public canonical URLs. `robots.txt` is crawl guidance, not security.

## Structured data

Structured data must reflect real public facts sourced from the Business object. Prefer a broad truthful local-business type over an inaccurate narrow subtype. Do not invent ratings, prices, hours, locations, service packages, or geography.

## Reviews

Only genuine customer reviews may appear. Preserve source/permission context where applicable. Do not write reviews for customers or manufacture aggregate ratings. Review requests should ask neutrally for experience, not a positive score.

## Credentials/insurance

Publish only confirmed factual language. Insurance wording, coverage limits, bonding status, and license status remain operational facts until confirmed. An expired trust record must not remain public accidentally.

## Regulatory boundaries

SEO must not create specialty trade claims beyond actual lawful/operational scope. An image of plumbing context does not authorize a “plumbing services” page.

## Future Project Records

Project Records should become the strongest long-term trust/search asset through actual conditions, accepted scope, work performed, result, genuine images, appropriate geography, and privacy-reviewed publication.

They are evidence objects first and SEO assets second.

## Project privacy

Do not expose client identity, exact address, unit number, tenant information, access/security data, billing information, or private property details unless permission and necessity justify it.

## Internal linking

Links should reflect semantic relationships such as Make-Readies → Vacant Unit Turn → Request Work. Use descriptive anchor text, not generic “Click here.”

## Open Graph/social

Major routes receive canonical title, description, URL, site name, and appropriate approved image. Default `SM-SOC-01` is derived deterministically from canonical identity + approved photography. Do not use malformed AI-rendered logos.

## Search Console and profiles

Launch should be ready for Search Console verification, sitemap submission, indexability checks, canonical review, and structured-data validation. Local profiles should use consistent real-world business name, website, phone, category, service areas, hours if genuinely established, and real imagery.

## NAP restraint

If there is no public address, do not invent one simply to satisfy “NAP.” Accurate Name + Phone + Service Area + Website is better than fictional location data.

## Content quality test

Before adding a search-oriented page, ask:

> Would this page still deserve to exist if Google did not?

If not, it probably does not belong.

## AI-generated SEO content

Generative systems may assist drafting internally, but publication authority remains human and factual. Do not mass-generate city pages, project stories, reviews, FAQ answers, or service claims.

## FAQ

No root FAQ page is required at launch. Answer questions where they naturally belong. Only create a dedicated FAQ if recurring questions justify it.

## Technical launch checklist

Verify HTTPS, canonical hostname, unique titles/descriptions, canonical tags, sitemap, robots, staging exclusion, semantic headings, factual business schema, centralized business data, social metadata/card, working internal links, tap-enabled contact links, correct 404 status, noindex confirmation page, private uploads, and Search Console readiness.
