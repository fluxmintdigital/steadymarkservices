## What SteadyMark is

SteadyMark is a **coordinated property-services operation**, not a generic handyman company. Its natural operating territories are:

1. Make-Readies & Turns
2. Maintenance & Property Support
3. Repairs & Punch Work
4. Recurring & Contract Support

Its higher-order value is reducing coordination burden by accepting the property-level problem or scope rather than forcing clients to classify every individual trade first.

Field capacity may scale through trusted, scope-matched support. Accountability remains anchored through Robert and SteadyMark.

> **Capacity may scale; accountability does not fragment.**

## Brand character

Steady, capable, organized, practical, human, professional without false scale, and direct without feeling cheap.

Visual direction centers property condition, finished work, real materials, documentary working context, warm working surfaces, graphite structure, restrained clay orientation marks, precise geometry, and minimal ornament.

## Documentary architecture

Photography should document:

**Context → Condition → Work → Result**

Future **Project Records** are the signature evidence object and use:

**Observed Condition → Accepted Scope → Work Performed → Result**

Synthetic launch imagery may represent capability and service context, but may not be presented as documentary proof of a specific completed SteadyMark job.

## Request Work

Request Work is a scope-intake instrument, not a generic contact form. It begins with the property need, accepts imperfect natural-language descriptions, and helps SteadyMark determine fit and next steps.

## Governing design fingerprint

Understand the real system first. Give important things a purpose and home. Expose enough structure to make truth legible. Use evidence over adjectives. Keep responsibility visible. Allow depth without requiring it. Let visual character emerge from the subject. Keep the shortest path to the visitor’s real objective obvious.
